# OnlyFans & Fansly agency glossary

The words agencies use every day, in plain language.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OFM** | OnlyFans management: running creators' accounts as an agency — chats, content plan, promotion, payouts. |
| **Agency** | A team that runs creators' accounts for a share of their earnings. |
| **Chatter** | A person who writes to fans on behalf of a creator, within the creator's rules: replies, keeps the conversation going, offers paid content. |
| **Team lead** | The person responsible for a shift: checks who is online, helps chatters, watches waiting fans. |
| **Shift** | A block of hours when certain chatters cover certain models. |
| **Handover** | What the outgoing chatter passes to the next one: open conversations, promises, notes. |
| **PPV** | Pay-per-view: a message with locked content that the fan pays to open. |
| **Mass message** | One message sent to many fans at once, often with a PPV. |
| **Vault** | The library of a creator's content that chatters can attach to messages. |
| **Whale** | A fan who spends far more than the others. |
| **Tip** | Money a fan sends without buying a specific piece of content. |
| **Subscription** | The monthly price a fan pays to follow a creator. |
| **Rebill** | An automatic subscription renewal; fans who turned rebill off may leave at the end of the period. |
| **Refund / chargeback** | Money returned to a fan, or disputed by the fan's bank. |
| **Reply time (median)** | How long fans typically wait for an answer; the median is less skewed by a few very slow replies than the average. |
| **Tracking link** | A link that shows which promotion a new fan came from. |
| **FYP** | "For You" page on Fansly: the discovery feed where new fans can find a creator. |
