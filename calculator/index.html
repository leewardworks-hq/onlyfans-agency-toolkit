Chatter payroll calculator

For OnlyFans and Fansly agencies. Enter hours, sales and pay terms for each chatter: the calculator adds hourly pay, fixed base, commission and bonus, and shows what each chatter really earned per hour. Everything stays in this browser.

  • Base pay = hours × hourly rate + fixed base. Commission = sales × commission % ÷ 100. Total = base pay + commission + bonus (a penalty is a negative bonus).
  • $ per hour = total ÷ hours. Use it to compare chatters who are paid on different terms.
  • Commission only? Leave the hourly rate and fixed base empty. Hours only? Leave the commission empty.
  • Prefer a spreadsheet? Use the template with the same formulas in Google Sheets or Excel.
  • Your data is saved only in this browser, so the table is still here when you come back.
