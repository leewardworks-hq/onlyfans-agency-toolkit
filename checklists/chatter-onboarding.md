# New chatter: the first week

Every new person asks the same questions in their first week. Answer them once, here.

## Before day one

- [ ] Decide which models the chatter will work on and in which shift.
- [ ] Give access to the chats **without sharing the model's password**. Whoever knows the password can take the account and the fans with it.
- [ ] Add the chatter to the team chat: the agency channel and their shift's group.
- [ ] Send the rules: tone, words that are not allowed, how to price PPV, what can never be promised.

## Day one

- [ ] Show where things are: the inbox, the content vault, the scripts, the shift start and end.
- [ ] Walk through one conversation together: notes about the fan, their past spend, what to offer.
- [ ] Explain how pay is counted: hours, commission, bonus (see [`templates/chatter-payroll.csv`](../templates/chatter-payroll.csv)).

## Days two to five

- [ ] Trial shifts next to an experienced chatter or with the team lead watching.
- [ ] Daily short feedback: reply time, PPV sent and bought, one thing to do better.
- [ ] Check that the shift handover is filled in (see [`shift-handover.md`](shift-handover.md)).

## End of week one

- [ ] Look at the numbers together: hours, sales, median reply time.
- [ ] Decide: keep, extend the trial, or part ways. Say it plainly.
