# Shift handover template

The next chatter should not start every conversation from zero. A fan should not feel that someone else is now writing. Copy this into your team chat at the end of every shift.

```
Shift: ______  Date: ______  Model(s): ______
Outgoing chatter: ______   Incoming chatter: ______

1. Open conversations (fan — where we stopped — what is next):
   -
   -

2. Promises made this shift (custom content, a reply "later today", a price):
   -

3. PPV sent and not bought yet (fan — what — price):
   -

4. Fans who spent the most this shift (and what they like):
   -

5. Problems: refunds, angry fans, a session that dropped, anything the team lead must know:
   -

6. Notes for the model or the manager:
   -
```

## Rules that make handovers work

- Plan a 15–30 minute overlap between shifts. That is the time for the handover.
- Write fan notes in one shared place, not in private messages between chatters.
- The team lead checks that the handover was posted before the outgoing chatter leaves.
