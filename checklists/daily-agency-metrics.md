# Daily agency numbers: what to check and when

If you find out about a bad day from the end-of-week report, the money is already gone. Check these in this order.

## Every morning (10 minutes)

- [ ] **Revenue yesterday and its mix:** PPV, subscriptions, tips, referrals, refunds. When the total drops, the mix tells you which stream moved.
- [ ] **Refunds separately.** A burst of refunds is a different conversation with the team than weak sales.
- [ ] **New fans, buyers, transactions — read them together:**
  - fewer new fans → look at traffic and tracking links (on Fansly, also discovery/FYP);
  - new fans steady but fewer buyers → fans come in and don't buy, that's a chat question;
  - buyers steady but fewer transactions → people buy less often, look at the average order and how many PPV went out.
- [ ] **Each platform separately** if you run both OnlyFans and Fansly.

## During every shift

- [ ] **Fans waiting for a reply.** A fan with no answer may leave without buying. Check this before revenue.
- [ ] **Median reply time** for the shift and for each chatter. If it went up, check who is on shift and whose conversations are piling up.
- [ ] **PPV sent and PPV bought** per chatter. Many sent and few bought means the offers miss.
- [ ] **Who is on shift but not online.** The team lead pings them first.

## Once a week

- [ ] Revenue per model and per chatter compared with last week.
- [ ] Hours and sales for payroll (see [`templates/chatter-payroll.csv`](../templates/chatter-payroll.csv)).
- [ ] Subscriptions expiring next week and the fans who spend the most ("whales").
- [ ] One thing to change next week, written down, with a name next to it.
